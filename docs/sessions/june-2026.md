### Report
The session began on clear spring night in Chamrousse, the 15th of Highspring. The player characters (PCs) found themselves listening to performances of Elvish poetry in [The Black Comedian](../locations/chamrousse/black-comedian.md) until they were interrupted by the arrival of a local man named William, who collapsed on the floor of the tavern with an arrow stuck in his back. 

Alara and Antoine carried the wounded man to the town infirmary where they met [Madame Geraldina](../people/Chamrousse/madame-geraldina.md), the town healer. Geraldina tells the good samaritans that many adventurers have met similar fates in the nearby [Castle Xyntillan](../locations/chamrousse/castle-xyntillan.md). Antoine recovered a scroll from William before learning he died from his wounds the next morning.

The PCs, now intrigued, decided to team up in order to learn more about the Castle. Probing the locals, they discovered that the Castle was once the seat of political power in Chamrousse. The Xyntillan family has since gone missing amidst rumors that they cling to immortality inside the walls of their home with some kind of powerful magic. Travelers of all kinds now flock to Chamrousse, seeking the family's alleged hoard of priceless treasures held deep within Castle Xyntillan's walls.

During their investigations, the characters encountered a number of colorful characters in Chamrousse:

* [Fernand Bonnel](../people/Chamrousse/fernand-bonnel.md), owner of The Black Comedian, its attached boarding house, and the local boat launch.
* [Etienne](../people/Chamrousse/etienne.md), a former mercenary with ties to [The Tap](../locations/chamrousse/the-tap.md) who now guards The Black Comedian.
* [Jacques Valt](../people/Chamrousse/jacques-valt.md), bristly owner of [The Chimera Apothecary](../locations/chamrousse/chimera-apothecary.md).
* [Madame Geraldina](../people/Chamrousse/madame-geraldina.md), the town healer, located at the infirmary attached to the [Temple of the Three Testaments](../locations/chamrousse/temple-of-the-three-testaments.md).
* [Princess Knife](../people/Chamrousse/princess-knife.md), whose adventuring party also seek the Castle's hoard.
* [Pascal Levesque](../people/Chamrousse/pascal-levesque.md), owner of the rough-and-tumble mercenary bar on the town's outskirts simply called [The Tap](../locations/chamrousse/the-tap.md).
* [Count Jean-Giscard Xyntillan](../people/Castle-Xyntillan/jean-giscard-xyntillan.md), the youngest Count of the Xyntillan family, living in self-imposed exile in the nearby town of Tourssevoy to avoid his family.

By paying Fernand for *The Special*, the party also revealed the following local rumors (which may or may not be true, warned Fernand!):

1. There's another Xyntillan named Jean. He is not the same man as Jean-Giscard Xyntillan. Do not get them mixed up, as it will anger both of them.
2. The Royal Tax Collectors would pay a reward for physical evidence that the Xyntillan hoard is real (implicating them in property tax avoidance).
3. Blerot the Woodsman swore to avenge his murdered lover, but disappeared in the Castle without a trace just a few months ago.
4. An old chimneysweep has told lurid tales of concealed shafts and secret doors in the fireplaces. A little soot won't hurt you!

Finally, the players discovered the following pieces of information about the Castle itself:

* A mercenary in The Tap told a tale of the [Blind Beast of Xyntillan](../people/Castle-Xyntillan/blind-beast.md), a monstrous, eyeless ape with the face and tail of a crocodile. It hides amongst the statues in the Castle's courtyards, pretending to be one.
* Those who attempt to climb the courtyard walls are assaulted by large, violent ravens.
* Shadowy figures stalk intruders from atop the Castle's walls.
* Those who have infiltrated the Castle's lakeside balcony entrance returned mad, repeating tales of "talking faces" that haunted them.
* Besides Blerot, the town's previous High Priest of the Three Testaments has also gone missing in Castle Xyntillan recently.
