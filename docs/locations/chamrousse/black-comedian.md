# The Black Comedian
A tavern with a small theater stage and a connected boarding house. The tavern's name comes from an actor who reportedly died during a performance many years ago.

## People
* [Fernand Bonnel](../../people/Chamrousse/fernand-bonnel.md) owns the establishment and lives upstairs
* [Etienne](../../people/Chamrousse/etienne.md) acts as the tavern's security and occassional server during busy hours

## Notes
* Fernand offers *The Special* - a handwritten note with a rumor about Castle Xyntillan or Chamrousse - for 5 coins. A disclaimer on each rumor protects Fernand from legal liability, should the rumor prove false.
* Fernand was using cut-rate flour to produce the tavern's infamously stale bread. [Chef](../../people/Player-Characters/chef.md) has taken over bread baking duties, much to Etienne's relief.
